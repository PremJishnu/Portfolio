My Story

I am a high school student with a growing interest in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, and engineering. I enjoy understanding how technology can be used to solve problems and create useful solutions.

Outside of academics and technology, I enjoy playing tennis, participating in leadership and community activities, mentoring other students, and learning from new experiences.

Why Computer Science?

For years, tennis has taught me to look for patterns. Before an opponent hits the ball, I pay attention to their position, movement, racket angle, and direction. I try to recognize patterns and predict what might happen next.

Programming gives me another way to look for patterns, break problems into smaller pieces, test ideas, and build solutions. Artificial Intelligence interests me because it takes this idea even further—using data and algorithms to recognize patterns and make useful predictions.
